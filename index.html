Craig A. Chikis

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Chicago and am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

My research lies at the intersection of macroeconomics, economic growth, and international trade. I study how firms, innovation, and the movement of people and ideas shape trade patterns, reallocation, and aggregate growth.

You can find my CV here. For questions, please contact me at cachikis@uchicago.edu.

Research

Job Market Paper

Old Roots, New Routes: The Trade Patterns of Immigrant-Founded Firms
with Berkay Saygin

This project studies the role of immigrant-founded firms in shaping international trade patterns, connecting founders’ roots to the markets their firms serve.
